## The Rhizomeur-Obsidian synergy 

While I believe that Rhizomeur *has the potential* to completely replace [[Obsidian]] (at least for me), it doesn't strictly *have* to (at least not for now). Because the structure of Rhizomeur is based on the existence of a database which would have table for links and a table for notes, Rhizomeur does not at all need Markdown links or frontmatter in order to function. This means something very special: One folder can be both a Rhizomeur knowledge graphe and an Obsidian vault. Thanks to this, I can focus on creating the things that Obsidian can't do, and I can relaxe on the creation of things it already does very well (like making a pretty list of all of my favorite artists). I don't have to change anything about how I already organize things with Obsidian, and I can simply add Rhizomeur on top of that as a parallel tool with different capabilities. I can even let Rhizomeur communicate with Obsidian for extra synergy!
