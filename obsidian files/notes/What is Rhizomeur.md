# The objective or Rhizomeur



While there are many tools which resemble Rhizomeur in name, theme, and functionality, Rhizomeur is my attempt to bring something new to life: I want to create the ultimate personal knowledge management system. Of course, what "the ultimate personal knowledge management system" even *is* is completely subjective, but that will be the guiding objective of this whole project. 

While I can think of many potential things that a tool like this could be used for, I need to remind myself I am note creating *the* tool, but simply *a* tool. Fist things first, Rhizomeur is a tool I am creating to fulfil a perceived lack of something which I have found in the personal knowledge space. I understand that there are tools which have similar capabilities to the ones that I am proposing--capabilities like querying sets semantically connected points of knowledge.
