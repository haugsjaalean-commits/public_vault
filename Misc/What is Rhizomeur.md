# The objective/direction or Rhizomeur: its identity

## Where does Rhizomeur stand?

When creating any sort of large scale (or even semi-large scale) project, it is important to have a cristal clear objective going into it. In the case of Rhizomeur, the objective is simple: ***create the ultimate personal knowledge management system***. This is my objective. I will address hereafter what this means to me. 

It has not escaped my attention that multiple tools already exists which exhibit similar capabilities to the one I'm envisioning. Quite ironically, I learned that someone even thought of the name before me: [Rhizomer](https://rhizomik.net/). This tool also has similar capabilities and purposes. From what I can tell, it is ment for exploring and understanding datasets based on semantic links. Although this resembles very closely my idea, it is far removed enough from the idea of personal knowledge management to be useless to me. I should not speak to soon, however, because it's certain that I can learn interesting things and gain new ideas by observing tools like Rhizomer. What I'm trying to say is that, although other tools approach mine, Rhizomeur has a specific purpose which has not been fulfilled by any other existing tool. 

It is important to note that I should not try to make a tool that improves upon existing tools in order to make *the perfect tool*; instead, I should only ever strive to keep to my original intentions as much as possible. I should, of course, try to create a tool which is as versatile and generalized as possible. In order to achieve this to the best of my abilities, I should do as much research as possible--research like trying to understand tools like Rhizomer.

The question of whether or not to build Rhizomeur on top of an already existing system has not escaped me either. I think that there are fair reasons to think that this would be a good idea, but I would wager that it isn't. Seeing as I have a very specific idea of what I want to achieve, starting from something that already exists can only bring me further from where I want to go. 

## What are the makings of a perfect personal knowledge management system?

Above, I stated that I wanted to create, "the ultimate personal knowledge management system". This is all well and good, but what does that even mean? Let me clear up this question and any other related question here. I will also expand on my wishes for this system. 

---

My vision for this system involves creating a tool which will aide in the tasks of knowledge management:
1. **Creating knowledge**
2. **Viewing knowledge**

These are obviously the two main things that any sort of editor needs to be  able to do, but it's important to realize that the task really is as simple as this. Of course, I could go into detail on the things that need to be put in place for *creating* and *viewing* to be satisfactory for me; in fact, I think I will do just that.

N.B. All of the decisions made when creating this system will be based on [[Public/The theory of information]]. 

### Creating knowledge

Creating knowledge entails creating Notes. In this system, Notes are knowledge. As stated in my theory, notes are all connected to each other via links.

N.B. As mentioned in [[Representing KUs]], notes do not represent things, but they implicitly represent notes about things. 

If I want creating knowledge to feel seamless in my system, I need the process of adding links and text and all of the other things which describe notes to be extremely easy and most importantly quick.

### Viewing knowledge


There are many different ways to view knowledge: knowledge can be viewed as text in a file, or as an item in a list, but the most important out of all of these methods is that of organization. Organization is the act of creating knowledge graphs. This is the absolute most important thing which I must succeed with this tool. 

I would also like to be able to create other tools which will aid in further contextualizing Notes. A tool for viewing the notes in a more Rhizomatic manner would be very nice. On top of this, it will obviously be necessary to be able to view notes on a more individual level, by seeing their text and links. 



## Final thoughts

I should try to understand as much as possible about the other tools which exists and surrounding areas of thought and research which could at all aid in my creation of as robust a system as possible. For example, I was reading about [[Ben Shneiderman's princes of information visualization and data analysis]], and I was getting inspired by it. I thought that I should maybe be rethinking some of my theories in the light of data analysis. This is just one example of many things which would be interesting to learn and think about before embarking on the journey of creating Rhizomeur.

Furthermore, Rhizomeur, for the moment, is simply something I'm creating to fill a gap that I noticed in the personal knowledge management space. I noticed that there was seemingly no clean system for organizing notes in a way that satisfied me--no unified idea of *what organization is and how we can do it*.
