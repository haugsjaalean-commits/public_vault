
## The situation

The user is a sculptor (Let's say that his name is Larkspur). He wants to use Rhizomeur in order to create a place for himself to organize his work and his inspirations and ideas for this work. 

First and foremost, he wants to create a way to organize his own works, projects, and ideas, so that he can always have a sense of what he's done and where he's going. 

Secondly, he wants a way to organize the things that are inspiring him, weather they are books, films, other sculptures, or simply thoughts and ideas. 

Thirdly, he wishes that he had a way of keeping track of particular streams of thought regarding his ideas. This means that he wants to be able to resemble all of the things I already mentioned into one place where he can see different dimensions and similarities throughout his works and inspirations. Basically, he seeks to create specific streams of inspiration for things he wants to incorporate in his work. For example, he has been interested in exploring more organic forms in his works, so he wants a way to gather inspiration and knowledge about more organic forms of sculpting all in one place. This also means that he needs a way to organize thins like different sculpting methods and practices.
