## The philosophy of a rhizome (according to me)


We have thoughts and minds. In the metaphor I'm about to make, it will help us to think of our minds as rhizome-like things. We see and feel things all the time, and we like it when we understand these things. Unfortunately, depending on who you ask, there is actually no way of ever understanding the things we experience every day. Despite this, our minds create paths and opinions--that's what they're for. Sometimes these paths work in our favor, and sometimes they don't, but we can always create new paths. A rhizome is not fixed to any given state; they can change, evolve, and produce new connexions, and so can we!
