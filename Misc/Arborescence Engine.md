## Thoughts

### Thoughts on data analytics 

I was thinking about data analytics, and I thought that, by understanding this field, I might find that there are certain things which I can incorporate into my design of Rhizomeur. While I have no claim to expertise in this field, I would wager that much of data analysis simply comes back to organization. It's just that the queries of data analytics are far more complexe. I don't think that data analytics is only organization; I think that it comes back to the other Engines as well. 

### Rethinking my philosophy of organization: to display or not to display 

I know that I want Organization to include the processes of *grouping* and *sorting*, but I don't know if I want it to include that of *displaying*. This is a tough choice that I don't have to make right now. I would say that, philosophically, *displaying* is one of the main parts of organization. I would also say that *displaying* will probably be aided by other Engines. 

## How it will work

### Queries



By the use of queries, the user is able to create branching search pathways. With the help of the [[Logic Engine]], these queries may contain information about the bidirectional links contained in the database, but they may also contain any other sort of logic. This means that markdown links can also be used in queries. 

#### Examples of queries

> Show me files with x or more number of words

>Show me files which have markdown links leading to `file_name.md`

>Show me files which are descendent from `file_name.md`
