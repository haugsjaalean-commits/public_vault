# OOF: Mapping [[The theory of information - public|my theory]] onto Obsidian 


## What is "OOF"?

I think that, to achieve the best results and avoid wasting time thinking, it is best to always have a guideline which describes how to form *organization*. I am going to number all of these guidelines for simplicity's sake. I am also going to call the guidelines "OOF", which stands for "==Obsidian Organization Philosophy==".

## The three dimensions of OOFs 


When conceptualizing an OOF, we want to think about optimizing these three things:
1. **Speed:** We want to be able to create notes quickly and effortlessly. 
2. **Organizational value:** How organized are the notes we create?
3. **Creation effort:** If the OOF, requires building an extremely complex plugin, then it becomes less ideal.

Often times, these three things are somewhat in opposition with each other. Spending time making a complex addon might increase speed and organizational value, but it will take more creation effort. If we increase speed, we are likely to loose organization, etc.
